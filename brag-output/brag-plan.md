## 🛠️ Verification & Rendering Engine

- **Preview Verification:** `test_preview.js` captures 13 multi-scene frame timestamps across the 56.50s timeline for instantaneous visual inspection.
- **Deterministic CDP Renderer:** `render_video.js` evaluates every millisecond deterministically through Chrome DevTools Protocol (`window.seekTo(t)`), streaming 1,695 JPEG frames at 30 fps into FFmpeg H.264 (`crf 18`, `+faststart`) with zero dropped frames.
