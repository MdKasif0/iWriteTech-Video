## 🎯 Creative Direction & Vision

**iWriteTech** is reimagined as an avant-garde editorial technology platform and hardware laboratory engineered for developers, designers, and focused creators. 

Rather than a simple slideshow, this film adopts the dynamic visual pacing, kinetic typography, camera depth, micro-interactions, and visual rhythm of top-tier motion design studios (Linear, Apple, Vercel).

### Core Motion Design Principles
1. **Kinetic Tokenization:** Code-style kinetic syntax chips floating in deep 3D space with subtle ambient bobbing and velocity blur streaks.
2. **Camera Choreography & Depth:** Layered parallax, dynamic scale, smooth cubic easing (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`), and simulated camera tilts.
3. **Micro-Interactions & Cursor Physics:** Animated smooth SVG cursor with realistic click easing, state flips, and coordinate targets.
4. **Editorial & High-Tech Contrast:** Rich warm studio canvas (`#F4EFE6`) shifting dynamically to deep obsidian darkroom (`#0C0D0E`), with electric blue (`#2563EB`) and emerald lab accents (`#10B981`).
