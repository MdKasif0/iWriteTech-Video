# iWriteTech — Launch Video Plan

## What is it?
A curated tech blog that handpicks aesthetic, minimal desk tech — keyboards, setups, accessories — so readers don't have to scroll through thousands of listings.

## Who is it for?
Desk setup enthusiasts, developers, and minimalists who care about tech that looks as good as it performs.

## What sets it apart?
100% hands-on tested. 100% editorially independent. No generic listicles — every pick is curated and aesthetic-first.

## The hook?
The hero headline itself: **"Tech that looks as good as it performs."** — paired with the warm, premium aesthetic of the site.

## Visual hook?
The golden-warm color palette + a clean desk setup image + typography that feels like a magazine, not a blog.

## Tone: `polished`
Serious, elegant, restrained. This isn't a joke project — it's a real editorial product. Let the aesthetic speak.

## One-line share caption:
*"Curated desk tech, tested by hand. iWriteTech."*

---

## Storyboard (20s total, 30fps, 1920×1080 landscape)

### Scene 1: Hook (0s–3s) — "The Question"
- **Black screen** → warm golden text fades in: **"Still scrolling Amazon for desk tech?"**
- Text holds 2s, then dissolves
- **Sound:** Low warm pad fades in

### Scene 2: Reveal (3s–7s) — "The Answer"
- Quick fade to warm cream background (#F4EFE6)
- iWriteTech logo animates in (typewriter-style)
- Below it, the tagline types out: **"Tech that looks as good as it performs."**
- Stat bar slides in from bottom: `100% Hands-On Tested · 100% Editorially Independent`
- **Sound:** Subtle whoosh on reveal, soft click on stats

### Scene 3: Highlight 1 (7s–11s) — "The Product"
- Smooth transition: a product card slides in from the right
- Shows the Featured Find card from the actual site with the desk setup image
- Category pills animate in: `Desk Setups · Keyboards · MacBook · Gaming`
- **Sound:** Soft transition swoosh

### Scene 4: Highlight 2 (11s–15s) — "The Experience"
- Split-screen morph: light mode → dark mode toggle
- Shows the beautiful reading experience
- Text overlay: **"Beautiful to read. Beautiful to browse."**
- **Sound:** Gentle toggle click

### Scene 5: Outro (15s–20s) — "The CTA"
- Fade to warm dark background (#0E0D0C)
- iWriteTech logo in gold (#C9A66B) centers with gentle scale-up
- Below: **"iwritetech.com"** fades in
- Final text: **"Curated. Tested. Aesthetic."**
- **Sound:** Music resolves, soft pad out

## Visual Identity (from the real site)
- **Light background:** #F4EFE6 (warm cream)
- **Dark background:** #0E0D0C (rich black)
- **Primary/Gold:** #866432 (light) / #C9A66B (dark)
- **Heading font:** Literata (serif)
- **Body font:** Geist Sans
