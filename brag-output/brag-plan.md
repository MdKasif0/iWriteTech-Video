## 🛠️ Verification & HyperFrames Rendering Architecture

- **Composition Engine:** `brag-output/composition/index.html` featuring deterministic time control via `window.seekTo(t)`:
  - 100% deterministic mathematical functions for all particle positions, 3D canvas projections, and typography animations.
  - Zero non-deterministic random state (`seed-based pseudo-random functions`).
  - No CSS keyframe timers or setIntervals; everything directly driven by time `t`.
- **Automated Verification:** `test_preview.js` captured 16 deterministic keyframe snapshots (`brag-output/work/test_previews_ref5/*.jpg`) across all 16 scenes for instant visual inspection.
- **Master CDP Headless Chrome Renderer:** `render_video.js` spawns headless Chromium at 1920×1080, steps frame-by-frame (4,700 frames total at 30 fps), captures raw JPEGs, and pipes directly to FFmpeg with `libx264` (`-crf 18`, `-preset medium`) muxed with `soundtrack_ref5.m4a`.
